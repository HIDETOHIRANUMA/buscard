5) Timetable data: each operator's GTFS-JP open data, downloaded at run time from the GTFS Data Repository (https://gtfs-data.jp/) or the Mobility Database. The license of each feed is shown in the app and on the printed sheet.

6) Map tiles: 地理院タイル（国土地理院） https://maps.gsi.go.jp/development/ichiran.html

7) Feed list (data/feeds_fallback.json): the feeds that are not in the GTFS Data Repository (for example ODPT),
the Mobility Database download addresses, and the whole list when the repository's API cannot be reached.
Made on 2026-11-06 from the Mobility Database catalog by MobilityData (feeds_v2.csv,
https://files.mobilitydatabase.org/feeds_v2.csv): for each Japanese GTFS feed, its id, operator, feed name,
prefecture, license URL and download URLs. The README of the catalog
(https://github.com/MobilityData/mobility-database-catalogs) says: "All of the Mobility Database catalog's
metadata is made available under Creative Commons CC0 (CC0)"
(https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/legalcode), and "Individual transit feeds are subject to
the terms & conditions of their own respective data provider." The entries describe feeds published through
the GTFS Data Repository (https://gtfs-data.jp/) and ODPT (https://www.odpt.org/). The timetables themselves are
not in this file; each keeps the license of its operator (see 5).
